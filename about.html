A Bangalore-based manufacturing and trading company focused on providing quality equipment and reliable service for construction and industrial applications.

Our Story

Built Around Trust & Product Quality

Samrat Tech Service and Solutions strongly emphasizes manufacturing, trading, electrical spares, and service solutions. With years of experience in the manufacturing and trading industry, we understand the unique requirements of our clients across construction, industrial and automation sectors.

We offer a comprehensive range of construction related products and services, from battery operated trolley and electrical spares to advanced automation systems and customized service solutions. Our experienced team of skilled professionals is committed to delivering exceptional customer service, reliable solutions, and high-quality products.

At Samrat Tech Service and Solutions, we strive to build long-term relationships with our customers by ensuring quality, timely delivery, technical expertise, and complete customer satisfaction in every project we undertake.
