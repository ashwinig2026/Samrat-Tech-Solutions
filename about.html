Our Story

Built Around Trust & Product Quality

Samrat Tech Solutions is a startup dealership supplying manufacturing products used primarily in construction — including vibratory machines, needle vibrators, and related industrial equipment.

Based in Peenya, Bangalore, we work with construction companies, contractors, builders, infrastructure project teams, and equipment dealers who need dependable products and clear commercial support.
